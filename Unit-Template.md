# Standard Unit Template (Adult-Friendly, Speaking-First)

Unit ရေးသူတိုင်း ဒီ quality gate ကိုလိုက်နာရမည်။ Level အလိုက် length/difficulty တိုးသော်လည်း section မလျှော့ရ။

## 1. Goal & Exit Task
- မြန်မာလို ရှင်းသော goal တစ်ခု
- တိုင်းတာနိုင်သော output: topic, partner, minutes, success condition

## 2. Quick Review (3–5 min)
- ယခင် unit chunks 3–5 ခုကို မကြည့်ဘဲပြောစေ

## 3. Useful Chunks (8–15)
- isolated words မဟုတ်ဘဲ reusable phrases
- meaning, natural example, register (formal/neutral/casual)

## 4. Language Point
- rule တစ်ခုသာ၊ examples 3–5 ခု၊ common Burmese-speaker mistake

## 5. Listening Lab
- Pre-listening prediction
- Script A: slow/clear
- Script B: natural speed/variation
- First listen: gist question
- Second listen: 3–5 detail questions/gap-fill
- Transcript နဲ့ answer key

## 6. Model Interaction
- 8+ turns; follow-up, clarification, repair ပါဝင်ရမည်

## 7. Controlled Mouth Practice (5–10 min)
- substitution/back-chaining/quick response

## 8. Guided Speaking (5–15 min)
- cue/support ပေးပြီး learner personalization

## 9. Information Gap / Role-play (8–20 min)
- A/B မှာ မတူသော information သို့မဟုတ် မတူသော goal
- မေး/ဖြေ/clarify မလုပ်ဘဲ task မပြီးနိုင်ရ

## 10. Free Speaking & Follow-ups
- notes မပါဘဲ Level 1: 1–3, L2: 3–5, L3: 5–8, L4: 8–12 minutes
- listener follow-up 2–5 ခု

## 11. Conversation Repair
`Could you say that again?` `Do you mean ...?` `Let me rephrase that.` စသည့် phrase အနည်းဆုံး 2 ခု

## 12. Pronunciation
- sound/stress/linking/intonation focus တစ်ခု
- listen-discriminate → repeat → sentence → spontaneous use

## 13. Recording & Feedback
- dated recording
- rubric criteria 2–3 ခုရွေး self/peer score
- Two Stars and One Step

## 14. Recycling
- 3-day, 7-day, 30-day retrieval prompt

## 15. Can-do & Exit Check
- [ ] measurable statements 3 ခု
- rubric score / completion condition
- Answer key

## Speaking-time Targets per Unit

| Level | Interaction + recording target |
|---|---:|
| 1 | 25–45 minutes |
| 2 | 45–75 minutes |
| 3 | 60–100 minutes |
| 4 | 90–150+ minutes |
