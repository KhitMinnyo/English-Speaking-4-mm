# AI-First Solo Practice Guide (v2) — AI နဲ့တစ်ယောက်တည်း လေ့ကျင့်နည်း

ဒီ v2 က traditional partner/teacher မရှိတောင် ChatGPT/Gemini စတဲ့ AI chat ကို “စကားပြောအဖော် + နောက်ခံဆရာ” အဖြစ်သုံးပြီး speaking ကိုနေ့တိုင်းတိုးတက်စေဖို့တည်ဆောက်ထားပါတယ်။ Burmese scaffolding နဲ့ adult-friendly microlearning ကိုမပြောင်းဘဲ AI ကို workflow အတွင်း ထည့်သွင်းပေးထားပါတယ်။

## Quick Start — Copy/Paste Starter Prompt

ဤစာသားကို ChatGPT/Gemini ထဲ paste လုပ်ပြီး စကားပြောစတင်ပါ (သင့် unit/level/topic ကိုချက်ချင်းပြောင်းရေးပါ)။

"""
You are my English speaking partner and coach for adult learners. Rules:
- Interaction-first: Keep turns short. Ask 2 follow-up questions every time.
- No mid-speech correction. Give feedback only after the task using "Two Stars and One Step".
- Use clear, natural English. If I type "မြန်မာ", briefly clarify in Burmese and go back to English.
- Respect timing: Warm-up 2 min, Listen 5, Mouth 5, Speak 5, Recall 3 (adjust if I say "short/long").
- For role-plays, keep hidden info and make me ask/clarify to succeed.
- Track my phrases (chunks) and recycle 3 old + 5–8 new each session.
- At the end, suggest a 3/7/30-day retrieval plan.
Context: Level = {Level 1/2/3/4}, Unit = {unit name}, Topic = {topic}. My goal: speak for {X} minutes about {personalization}.
Start with: a 2‑minute warm-up quiz using yesterday’s 3 chunks. Keep it conversational.
"""

Tip: Session တစ်ခုစတင်တိုင်း အပေါ်က prompt ကို paste → level/unit/topic ကို ပြောင်းပါ။

## 20-Minute Solo Session (AI-integrated)

1) 2 min — Warm-up (AI Quiz)
- AI ကိုမနေ့က chunks 3 ခုနဲ့ Quick Q&A လုပ်စေပါ။
- Prompt: "Warm-up quiz with yesterday’s 3 chunks. One question at a time. I answer by speaking; then briefly confirm and move on."

2) 5 min — Listen (Model Input)
- Option A: Repository ထဲက Audio-Scripts သုံးပါ။ Script A (slow) → Script B (natural) အစဉ်နားထောင်ပါ။
- Option B: AI ကို slow/clear + natural variation dialog တစ်ခုပေးရန် တောင်းပါ။
- Prompts:
  - "Create a short dialog (10–12 lines) on {topic}: version A = slow/clear; version B = natural. Include gist question and 3–5 detail questions."
  - "Read me version A slowly. Stop after each line so I can shadow."

3) 5 min — Mouth Training (AI Coach)
- Back-chaining, substitution, quick response ကို AI ထံ တောင်းပါ။
- Prompts:
  - "Give 5 short lines from our chunks for back-chaining. Pace me and wait after each line."
  - "Substitution drill: rotate time/place/person on these 5 chunks: {list}."

4) 5 min — Speak (Guided → Personal)
- Cue-based speaking; AI က follow-up 2 ခုမေးပါ။
- Prompts:
  - "Guide me to speak about {my life/work/example} for 3–5 minutes. Ask 2 follow-up questions every time. No correction until the end."
  - "Now raise difficulty slightly (faster pace, less support)."

5) 3 min — Recall (Spaced Retrieval)
- စာမကြည့်ဘဲ chunk 3 + sentence 3 ပြန်ပြောပါ။
- Prompt: "Test me: 3 phrases + 3 sentences from today (no text). After each, show a model answer only if I ask."

Minimum day (ခက်တဲ့နေ့):
- AI နဲ့ audio 1 မိနစ် → shadow sentence 3 ကြောင်း → ကိုယ့်အကြောင်း 3 ကြောင်း record။ Streak မပြတ်စေရန် OK။

## Weekly Pace (AI-ready)
- Easy: တစ်ပတ် 4 ရက် × 15–20 min; 2 weeks per unit
- Standard: တစ်ပတ် 5 ရက် × 25–35 min; 1 week per unit
- Intensive: တစ်ပတ် 6 ရက် × 45–60 min; 1 week per unit + extra free talk

AI နဲ့ရတဲ့ အားသာချက်: partner မလို၊ timing/prompt ကိုမိမိ ထိန်းနိုင်၊ immediate follow-up/variation ပြုလုပ်နိုင်။

## Feedback Protocol (Two Stars and One Step)
- Session အဆုံးတွင် AI ကို ဤသို့တောင်းပါ: "Give Two Stars and One Step: 2 positives and 1 focused improvement (pronunciation/fluency/chunks). Provide 1 micro-exercise for tomorrow."
- Mid-speech correction မထည့်ရန် သတိပေးရန်: "Don’t interrupt—feedback after task."

## Role-play & Information Gap (Solo)
- Prompt: "Run an A/B role-play about {scenario}. Keep your information secret. Make success impossible unless I ask good questions and clarify. Give me a score (0–3) for asking, clarifying, and confirming."
- Prompt (progressive difficulty): "Repeat the role-play faster with 2 unexpected turns (schedule change/problem)."

## Pronunciation Focus (Burmese-friendly)
- Prompt: "As a pronunciation coach for Burmese speakers, target 1 item (e.g., /θ/ vs /t/, stress, linking). Give listen-discriminate → repeat → sentence → spontaneous task. No phonetic walls of text—short cues only."
- Prompt (minimal pairs): "Generate 6 minimal pairs for {sound}, common Burmese confusions, with example sentences."

## Recording & Evidence
- Naming: L{level}-U{unit}-D{YYYY-MM-DD}-{topic}.m4a
- Prompt (rubric): "Evaluate my recording using 3 criteria: Intelligibility, Fluency, Use of target chunks (0–5). Two Stars and One Step. Be kind, precise, brief."
- Repository rubric: Assessments/Speaking-Rubric.md ကိုလည်းကြည့်ပါ။

## Recycling (3/7/30/90)
- Prompt: "Schedule my spaced retrieval: today, +3d, +7d, +30d, +90d with 3 prompts each. At each review, recycle 3 old + 2 new variations."

## Safety & Stress Reduction
- Message-first: ပြောနေစဉ် meaning ရောက်/မရောက်သာအရင်စစ်—form ကို task အပြီး address ပါ။
- Visibility/hearing difficulty: "Use larger text, slow TTS, allow response time."
- Privacy: Real names/data မခွင့်လှစ်ပါ—role-play data ထုတ်ယူရန် AI ကို fictitious info only လို့ပေးပါ။

## Troubleshooting
- AI reply လိုငယ်တာ: "Shorter please. One turn at a time."
- AI correction မရပ်တာ: "Hold feedback until the end."
- Topic မတော်တာ: "Switch to {new topic}; keep 2 follow-up questions each turn."
- Too easy/too hard: "Increase/Reduce difficulty one step (speed/support/vocab)."

## Daily Checklist
- [ ] Warm-up 2 min
- [ ] Listen 5 min (slow → natural)
- [ ] Mouth training 5 min (5 lines)
- [ ] Speak 5 min (guided → free)
- [ ] Recall 3 min (3 phrases + 3 sentences)
- [ ] Record weekly evidence + rubric

Happy solo speaking—partner မလိုပဲ consistency နဲ့ usable speech ကို တိုင်းနိုင်ဖို့ ဒီ v2 ကို သုံးပါ။
