## Script

> Last Tuesday, Maya had an appointment at a health clinic at half past nine. She left home early, but the bus broke down on the way. She called the clinic to explain that she might be twenty minutes late. The receptionist first suggested changing the appointment to Friday. Maya explained that she had already taken time off work and asked whether the doctor could still see her that morning. After checking the schedule, the receptionist offered a ten-thirty appointment. Maya accepted it, thanked her, and decided to take a taxi for the rest of the journey. Although the delay cost her extra money, she was relieved that she did not have to miss another day of work.

## Expected evidence

- Main idea: transport problem caused a late medical appointment; Maya negotiated a later time
- Details: Tuesday; 9:30; bus broke down; called clinic; Friday suggested; 10:30 accepted; taxi; work concern
- Suitable clarification questions: “Why couldn’t she go on Friday?”, “How late did she expect to be?”, etc.

Score listening separately:

- 1: isolated detail only
- 2: partial main idea, several incorrect/missing details
- 3: correct main idea and 3–4 details
- 4: main idea, most details, reasonable retell
- 5: accurate retell plus inference and relevant clarification
